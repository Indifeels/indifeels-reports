All periods

Yesterday, 7 and 15 days compare with the last 30 days. The 30 days column compares with the previous 30 days (__PREV__). Chips are percentage points of share; totals compare sales per day.

  • RAG rules. Rank: 1–2 green, 3 amber, 4 red. Profit: margin on revenue excluding GST, 40% and above green, 15–40% amber, under 15% red. ROAS: 3.0x and above green, 1.0–3.0x amber, under 1.0x red. Share of revenue or profit: 25% and above green, 10–25% amber, under 10% red.
  • Profit = revenue ÷ 1.1 (remove GST), minus 25% product cost, minus ad spend and management fees. No-spend sources have no deductions after product cost.
  • ROAS = revenue ÷ spend, with management fees included. Revenue is the Shopify order total after refunds and edits.
  • Facebook non-tracked = FB WhatsApp, FB/IG Page messages and FB Calls. Facebook tracked = FB Online Orders – WEB. Google tracked = Google Online Orders – WEB.
  • Organic = Organic Orders, Google calls (GMB), Referral SEO and the GMB share of store visits. No-spend = Marketplace, Word of Mouth, Direct, Repeat Customer and unassigned.
  • Store visits are split by direction counts, using the 30-day ratio (__ADS__% Google Ads, __GMB__% GMB), because recent GMB data is incomplete.
  • Fixed costs per day: Google management $670/month, Facebook management 15,000 INR ÷ 67 per month, organic/SEO $250/month, each ÷ 30. Platform fees are split between tracked and non-tracked by ad spend.
  • __WINDOWS__. Today's orders are excluded.
  • Yesterday has few orders, so its numbers swing hard. Read 7 and 15 days for direction.
