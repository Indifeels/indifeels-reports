Logic

Published date = custom.published_date_old metafield for migrated products (created before 18 Apr 2026 11pm UTC), otherwise created date. Sales = all-time line-item quantity (Variant ID for variants, Product ID for product level). Days to Sell 1 Unit = days since published ÷ units sold. Days to stockout = current stock × variant days-to-sell. Required stock = CEILING(28 ÷ Days to Sell 1 Unit) × leniency (1→1×, 2→1.2×, 3→1.4×, 4→1.6×, 5→1.8×, 6→2×, 7→2.5×, 8→3×, 9→4×, 10→5×), rounded up. To order = max(0, required − stock). Priority view ranks every selling variant across all products by days to stockout (out-of-stock fast sellers first) and repeats a product header each time the ranking switches product. Size view groups by product, then colour, then size. Save sends the quantity to the supplier (locked once the supplier adds it to the box). “Stock More Variants” = on market ≥ 3 months, product days-to-sell < 50, ≥ 3 units sold.

This mirrors what the supplier sees: the product photo, the variant and the quantity to restock. When they press Add to box or Ship it shows here within a minute (and in My view).
