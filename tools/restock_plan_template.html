Logic

Published date = custom.published_date_old metafield for migrated products (created before 18 Apr 2026 11pm UTC), otherwise created date. Sales = all-time line-item quantity (Variant ID for variants, Product ID for product level). Days to Sell 1 Unit = days since published ÷ units sold. Days to stockout = current stock × variant days-to-sell. Required stock = CEILING(28 ÷ Days to Sell 1 Unit) × leniency (1→1×, 2→1.2×, 3→1.4×, 4→1.6×, 5→1.8×, 6→2×, 7→2.5×, 8→3×, 9→4×, 10→5×), rounded up. To order = max(0, required − stock). Eligible (green) = the product sells 1 unit in under 50 days; Not eligible (red) = 50 days or more. Eligible products always come first, whatever the sort; you can still restock a not-eligible one by hand. Priority ranks every selling variant across all products (a product header repeats each time the ranking switches product) by the units it is short over the 28-day lead time, adjusted so noise does not dominate: ×confidence (a product needs ~10 sales and ~60 days before its sell rate is trusted, so brand-new products rank lower), ×category weight (categories with a small share of total sales, e.g. footwear, rank lower; share ≥ 25% = full weight), ×spike damping (if the last 30 days sell much faster than the lifetime rate, the extra is discounted, more strongly for seasonal kids/boys lines). Hover a rank to see its score. Ties fall back to days of stock left. By date lists variants by most recent sale. Size groups by product, then colour, then size. The other sorts group by product. Order / top-ups: “To order” = required − stock − units already on order or on the way. Save creates a new order line for the supplier (several can exist for the same variant); × cancels one the supplier has not boxed yet. Received clears one shipment line; whole box received (Boxes on the way) clears every line in that box. Discontinued is saved here and written to the Shopify field custom.discountinued on the next refresh; discontinued products are hidden and no longer counted for restocking. “Stock More Variants” = on market ≥ 3 months, product days-to-sell < 50, ≥ 3 units sold.

The master list and each supplier's boxes, read-only. Boxes are named by the supplier (Pankaj = P1, Vistara = V1 …). Changes show here within a minute.
